Our Story

How Noor-ul-Iman Was Born

The Problem We Saw

Most Islamic apps felt outdated, cluttered with ads, or scattered across multiple downloads. A brother wanted prayer times in one app, Quran in another, Tasbih in a third. Each app pestered users with ads — sometimes even on Quran pages, which felt deeply wrong.

Our Solution

We built Noor-ul-Iman with a simple promise: everything a Muslim needs in one beautifully crafted app. Designed like a billion-dollar product. Free forever. No ads on Quran pages. Available in your language. Works offline. Made with love for the global Ummah.
