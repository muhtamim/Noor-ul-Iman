Privacy Policy

Your privacy is sacred to us — Last updated: May 2026

Our Privacy Promise

We do not sell your data. We do not track you across the web. We collect minimum information needed to provide our services. Your worship is between you and Allah — we keep it that way.

No Account Required
Use the entire app without signing up. We don't need to know who you are to help you pray.
Local Storage Only
Your bookmarks, settings, streaks stay on YOUR device. Never uploaded anywhere.
Transparent APIs
We use public Islamic APIs. All sources listed at the bottom of this page.

1. What We Collect

We collect nothing personal about you. No name, no email, no phone number — unless you voluntarily contact us. The app stores certain data locally on your device (in your browser's localStorage):

  • Theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • Language preference for Quran translation
  • Selected reciter
  • Tasbih counter & dhikr count
  • Prayer streak history
  • Bookmarked Quran verses
  • Azan notification on/off
  • Approximate location (only if you grant permission, to calculate prayer times)
This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server. Clear your browser data to remove it completely.

2. Location Data

When you use the Prayer Times or Qibla features, your browser asks for permission to share your location. If granted:

  • Your coordinates are sent to Aladhan API (free Islamic API) to calculate accurate prayer times
  • Coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap for reverse geocoding (to display your city name)
  • We do not store your location on our servers
  • You can deny location access anytime; the app will fallback to Dhaka, Bangladesh by default

3. Third-Party Services

We use these public APIs to provide Islamic content. Each may have its own privacy policy:

  • alquran.cloud — Quran text & translations
  • aladhan.com — Prayer times & Hijri dates
  • islamic.network — Quran audio CDN
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — Reverse geocoding
  • mp3quran.net — Quran audio streams
  • YouTube — Live Makkah/Madinah streams (we use youtube-nocookie domain for privacy)

4. Analytics & Tracking

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any tracking scripts. No advertising cookies. No tracking pixels. No third-party analytics.

5. Donations & Payments

When you donate via PayPal, Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub Sponsors, bKash, Nagad, or Rocket — those are external services. They have their own privacy policies. We only receive the donation amount, not your full payment details.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for children. Since we collect no personal information, there's nothing to protect — kids can safely use the app to learn Quran without parental concerns.

7. Your Rights

  • Right to access: All your data is in your browser's localStorage — check it via DevTools anytime
  • Right to delete: Clear your browser cache to delete all data instantly
  • Right to opt-out: You can deny location & audio permissions; the app still works
  • Right to contact us: Email us anything at any time

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the change here with a new "Last Updated" date. Major changes will be announced on the homepage.

9. Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Reach out via our Contact page. We respond within 48 hours, Insha'Allah.
