Quran Shikkhanur Bebostha — From alphabet to fluent reading

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

"My Lord, increase me in knowledge" — Quran 20:114

Step 1: Alphabet
Learn all 28 Arabic letters with examples & pronunciation.
Step 2: Harakat
Learn vowel marks (Fatha, Kasra, Damma) to read words.
Step 3: Tajweed
Master pronunciation rules for proper recitation.
Step 4: Memorize
Start memorizing short surahs with audio.
📌 Tip: Click any letter to hear its pronunciation. The Arabic alphabet has 28 letters and is written from right to left.
📌 What are Harakat? Arabic letters don't have built-in vowels. Harakat (diacritical marks) tell you how to pronounce each letter. Master these to read any Arabic word!
📌 What is Tajweed? Tajweed means "to improve" — it's the science of correctly pronouncing the Quran. These rules ensure each letter is pronounced from its proper place with proper attributes.
📌 Memorization Tips: Start with the shortest surahs. Listen to the audio multiple times before reading. Repeat each verse 10 times daily. Aim to memorize 1 short surah per week.
📌 Why learn vocabulary? Just 100 words make up over 50% of the Quran! Learn these high-frequency words to understand the Quran while reading.
