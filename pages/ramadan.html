Ramadan Calendar

Sehri & Iftar times for the holy month of Ramadan

شَهْرُ رَمَضَانَ الَّذِي أُنزِلَ فِيهِ الْقُرْآنُ

"The month of Ramadan in which the Quran was revealed" — Quran 2:185

Sehri (Suhoor)
The pre-dawn meal before Fajr. Eating Sehri is a Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.
Iftar
Breaking the fast at sunset (Maghrib). Best to break with dates and water.
Taraweeh
Special night prayers after Isha. A blessed time for reflection and Quran.

Dua for Sehri (Niyyah)
وَبِصَوْمِ غَدٍ نَّوَيْتُ مِنْ شَهْرِ رَمَضَانَ
Wa bisawmi ghadinn nawaiytu min shahri ramadan
I intend to keep the fast for tomorrow in the month of Ramadan.
Dua for Iftar (Breaking Fast)
ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ
Dhahaba-z-zama'u wabtallatil-'uruqu wa thabatal-ajru insha'Allah
The thirst is gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, if Allah wills.
— Abu Dawud 2357
Ramadan Essentials

Make the Most of Ramadan

Recite Quran Daily
Aim to complete the Quran. Recite at least 1 Juz (Para) per day to finish in 30 days.
Increase Dua
The dua of a fasting person is not rejected. Especially at the time of Iftar.
Give Charity (Sadaqah)
Charity in Ramadan is multiplied. The Prophet ﷺ was most generous in this month.
